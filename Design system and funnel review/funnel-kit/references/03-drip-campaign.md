# Drip Campaign Copy

Four sequences. Written in Ryan's voice — plain, short, no marketing language.
Send from a personal address (`ryan@websitecalgary.com`), plain text, no template
graphics. It should look like an email from a guy, because it is.

**Formatting rules:** short paragraphs. No images in sequences 1–3. One link per
email. Sign every one the same way.

# SEQUENCE 3 — Long-term nurture (said no, or went cold)
Monthly, 90 days. Value only. Nothing sold.

## Month 1
**Subject:** Three things costing Calgary businesses calls

Short, genuinely useful email. No pitch.

1. Your Google listing hours don't match your website hours. Google notices.
2. Your contact form goes to an email nobody checks. Test it right now.
3. Your site takes six seconds to load on LTE. Half your visitors are gone
   before it finishes.

All three are free to fix yourself. Reply if you want to know how.

Ryan

## Month 2
**Subject:** People are asking AI for contractor recommendations now

Explain plainly that customers are increasingly asking ChatGPT and Claude "who's
a good plumber in Calgary," and that most local businesses are invisible to those
systems. Explain what makes a business visible. Do not pitch. The education
*is* the pitch.

## Month 3
**Subject:** Still here

One paragraph. No offer. "Twenty years, still in Calgary, still building. If your
site ever starts bugging you, you know where I am."

## Day 1 — Live
**Subject:** You're live

Link. Screenshot. One line on how to request a change. Ask for nothing yet.

## Day 3 — First change prompt
**Subject:** Send me something to change

Get them to use the queue immediately. A client who has used the change system
once churns dramatically less than one who never has.

## Day 7 — Review request
**Subject:** Would you do me a favour?

Ask for the Google review while the launch feeling is fresh. This is the single
best window you'll get.

## Day 14 — Growth upsell
**Subject:** Now let's get you found

Pitch the Growth plan. The site works; now the problem is traffic.

## Day 45 — Brand film upsell
**Subject:** An idea for you

Pitch the Brand Story Film. This is where the Seeff / Taylor / Black endorsements
actually earn their place — a contractor cannot get near that production pedigree
anywhere else, at any price.

## Day 90 — Referral ask
**Subject:** Know anyone?

Offer a real incentive: one month free for them, and something for the referral.
Trades refer trades — this is the cheapest acquisition channel available.
