# The Offer Ladder

Tripwire → Core → Bump → Upsell → Continuity, with a downsell at every refusal point.

---

## 1. TRIPWIRE — The 24-Hour Website Teardown · $47

**What they get:** a personalized 8–12 minute screen-recorded video walking
through their actual website and Google Business Profile, showing exactly what's
costing them phone calls — plus a one-page fix list. Delivered within 24 hours.

**Why this one:**

- It uses Ryan's real edge — he's a production guy, so a recorded teardown costs
  him 20 minutes and looks like a $500 deliverable
- It's a **buyer filter.** Someone who pays $47 has proven they'll transact
- **The teardown *is* the sales call.** By the time they finish watching, they
  have a list of everything wrong and one obvious person to fix it
- It's instantly deliverable, so it can convert cold traffic same-day

**The close, said at the end of every teardown video:**

> "That's the list. You can take it to whoever you like — it's yours either way.
> Or I can just do all of it, and have the new site live by this time tomorrow.
> Your $47 comes off the rush fee. Link's below."

**Credit mechanic:** the $47 applies against the $497 rush fee. Makes the
tripwire feel free in hindsight and creates urgency to convert.

**Target:** 20–30% of teardown buyers should convert to Core within 14 days. If
you're under 10%, the teardown isn't ending with a strong enough close.

### Tripwire downsell — Free Teardown, 60 seconds

For anyone who won't pay $47: an auto-generated preview of their homepage rebuilt.
No human time. Captures the email, enters the drip. Lower quality lead, but free
to produce.

## 2. CORE OFFER — $497 rush fee + $147/mo

Covered in `SKILL.md`. This is the anchor everything else hangs off.

### ORDER BUMP (at checkout) — Google Business Profile Rescue · +$97

One checkbox on the payment page:

> **Add Google Business Profile Rescue — $97**
> Most Calgary businesses are losing calls to competitors with better-optimized
> map listings. We'll fix your categories, services, photos, hours and posting
> in the same 24 hours. One time, not monthly.

**Why it works:** it's the single highest-intent add-on for a local service
business, it costs ~30 minutes to deliver, and it's the natural on-ramp to the
Growth plan. Expect 30–40% take rate. That's roughly $30–40 added to every sale
for almost no marginal cost.

## 3. UPSELLS (in sequence)

### Upsell A — Growth plan · $147/mo → $497/mo
**When:** immediately post-purchase, and again at day 30.
**Pitch:** "The site's live. Now let's make sure people find it — Google, maps,
reviews, and the AI assistants people are actually asking for recommendations
now."
**Target:** 20–25% upgrade within 60 days.

### Upsell B — The Brand Story Film · $2,500–$4,500
**When:** day 45–90, once trust is established.
**This is the profit centre and the moat.**

Nobody else in the $147/mo website market can offer this. Ryan has real
production capability and endorsements from Norman Seeff, Renée Taylor and
Jully Black. A Calgary contractor cannot get near that anywhere else at any
price.

**Pitch:** "Your competitors all have the same stock photos. You'd have a real
film. Two hours of your time, one shoot day, and you own it forever."

**Target:** 5–8% of the client base per year. At 100 clients that's 5–8 films —
$15,000–$35,000 on top of recurring.

### Upsell C — Ads Management · $1,000/mo + spend
**When:** only after the site is converting and reviews are flowing. Selling ads
into a site that doesn't convert is how you lose a client.

### Upsell D — Multi-location / second site · $297 rush + $97/mo each
For clients with more than one brand, location, or service line. Near-zero
acquisition cost.

---

## 4. DOWNSELLS

Every "no" gets a smaller "yes." Never let someone leave at zero.

### Downsell A — Standard build, 7 days, no rush fee
**Trigger:** price resistance on the $497.
Same site, same plan, they just wait. **Already on the landing page.** Converts
the price-sensitive buyer into a $147/mo customer instead of a bounce.

### Downsell B — Website Rescue · $297 one-time
**Trigger:** "I'm not ready to replace my site."
Keep their existing site. Fix the five things costing them the most — mobile,
speed, contact form, calls-to-action, Google listing. One-time, no subscription.

**Why it's the best downsell here:** it takes cash today from someone who was
about to leave, and it puts Ryan inside their site. A meaningful share come back
for the full offer within six months, because now they trust him and he already
has access.

### Downsell C — Split the rush fee · 2 × $249
**Trigger:** cash-flow objection, not value objection. Second payment on day 30.

### Downsell D — Lights On · $97/mo, no rush fee
**Trigger:** last resort before they walk.
Hosting, security, backups, uptime, **two changes a month** (not unlimited).
Deliberately worse than Core so it doesn't cannibalize — the two-change cap is
what makes people upgrade.

### Downsell E — The Reminder
**Trigger:** total no.
They stay on the drip. Nothing is sold. Sequence 3 in `03-drip-campaign.md`
runs for 90 days.
