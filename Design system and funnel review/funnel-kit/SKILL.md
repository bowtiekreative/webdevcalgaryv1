# Website Calgary — Funnel System

## What this is

A local-market funnel selling **websites that go live in 24 hours, with unlimited
changes forever and a price that never increases**, to Calgary small business
owners. Operated by Ryan Perez / Bow Tie Kreative, Calgary AB.

The build is AI-assisted. Ryan is the operator, not the labourer — he directs the
build and owns the ongoing relationship. **The product being sold is not the
website. It is someone reliably owning the website.**

## Core positioning

> Anyone can get a website built now. Almost nobody can get one that is still
> correct, still live, and still maintained two years from now.

Three claims, in this order of importance:

1. **Live tomorrow** — 24-hour turnaround, guaranteed with a refund
2. **Never goes stale** — unlimited changes, 48h turnaround, no change fees
3. **Price locked for life** — never increases, no contract, they own everything

## The avatar

**Primary — "the owner-operator."** 38–55, runs a Calgary trades or home-services
business (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping/snow, concrete,
renovation, garage doors). 2–12 staff. Reads on a phone, often in a truck.
Their site was built 4–7 years ago or never. Decides fast, hates being sold to,
allergic to jargon and to feeling locked in.

**Secondary — "the professional."** 32–50, clinic / med spa / dental / law /
accounting / studio / consultant. More design-sensitive, longer consideration,
higher upsell ceiling.

**Market size:** ~57,900 businesses in Calgary as of 2025, ~93.8% of them small.
Population ~1.68M, growing ~4%/yr. Median household income among the highest of
any major Canadian city.

**Service radius:** Calgary plus Airdrie, Okotoks, Cochrane, Chestermere,
Strathmore.

capability, which none of the above can match.

## Pricing (current)

**Entry — 24-hour rush fee: $497.** Framed as a rush fee, not a build fee. The
build is included; what costs money is jumping the queue. Standard 7-day build is
available with no rush fee — this exists to convert price-resistant buyers rather
than lose them.

**Core — $147/mo.** Unlimited changes (48h), hosting, SSL, domain, backups,
security, monitoring. Client owns everything. Price locked for life.

**Growth — $497/mo.** Core plus Google Business Profile management, local SEO,
review automation, AI-search visibility, monthly plain-English report.

**Unit economics:** carry is roughly $21/mo per site on static infrastructure
(hosting ~$3–5, update labour amortized, tooling). Gross margin at Core ≈
$126/mo. **This assumes ~2 hrs/yr of update labour per client and is untested —
track actual hours from client one.** If real usage runs 6 hrs/yr, margin drops
toward $50 and the queue guardrail below becomes load-bearing.

**Do not run this on per-site managed WordPress.** It triples the carry and
destroys the model. Static/Astro/Next on cheap stacked infrastructure only.

## Guardrails that make "unlimited" survivable

- **Queue, one active request at a time**, 48h turnaround. This single mechanic
  is what makes unlimited viable.
- **Scope fence:** copy, images, sections, pages, security, uptime = included.
  Rebrand, ecommerce, custom integrations, apps = quoted separately.
- **Non-transferable** — the lifetime price lock dies if the business is sold.
- **Page band** — up to 10 pages included, extras billed.
- **Termination clause** for abuse.

## Guarantee mechanics

> Live in 24 hours or the rush fee is refunded — and you keep the site.
> Then 30 days to cancel and still keep the site and domain.

- Cutoff is **2pm MT**. After 2pm, the clock starts 9am next business day.
- Weekend orders start Monday 9am.
- **Send the exact go-live timestamp in writing the moment payment clears.**
  Ambiguous deadlines are the only thing that turns this guarantee into a
  support problem.

## "Do I get to keep the site?" — the answer

Yes. Always. No hedging, no fine print. The domain, the content, and an exported
static bundle all go with them. What they give up by leaving is the maintenance,
not the asset. Any hesitation here makes you sound like the guy who held their
last site hostage — which is the exact wound the offer sells against.

## Voice rules

- Plain, direct, short sentences. Calgary owner-operators are allergic to
  marketing language.
- Never say "solutions," "leverage," "elevate," "digital presence," "synergy."
- Say the number out loud. Never "contact us for pricing."
- Name the objection before they do — the fine print, the lock-in, the ghosting.
- One restrained local nod at most. Heavy Stampede/cowboy theming reads as
  cheesy to people who actually live here year-round.
- Contractions always. Write it the way you'd say it on the phone.
