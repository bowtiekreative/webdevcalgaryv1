06 · Voice & rules

Say

Plain, direct, short sentences. Contractions always. Say the number out loud. Name the objection first — the fine print, the lock-in, the ghosting. One restrained local nod at most.

Never say

"Solutions." "Leverage." "Elevate." "Digital presence." "Synergy." "Contact us for pricing." Heavy Stampede/cowboy theming.

Never show

Stock photos of smiling people in hardhats. Gradients. Rounded "friendly" corners. Authority proof standing alone — peer proof first, always.
