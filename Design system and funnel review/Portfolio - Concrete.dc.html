Why concrete fails in Calgary

Sixty freeze-thaw cycles a winter. Concrete fails here for three reasons — and all three are invisible on pour day.

Bad base

We compact 6" of gravel in lifts and test it. Skipping this is why driveways sink at the apron.

Wrong mix

32 MPa, air-entrained, every exterior pour. It costs more per metre. It's not optional.

Rushed cure

Cure compound, sealed at 28 days, and we come back to do it. That return visit is in the quote.
