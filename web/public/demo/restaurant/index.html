The story

Two cooks, one grill, forty seats.

We spent fifteen years in other people's kitchens — Rouge, a stint in Copenhagen, a food truck that broke down more than it drove. Then this room in Inglewood came up for lease and we stopped talking about it.

Everything is cooked over Alberta birch. The menu is whatever was good this week. That's the restaurant.
